# Voltway Customer FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Orders within Morocco ship from our Casablanca warehouse and arrive in 2 to 4 business days. Orders to the EU take 5 to 8 business days. We do not currently ship outside Morocco and the EU.

## Do I need a license to ride a Voltway V2?

No. The V2 is a pedelec: the motor only assists while you pedal and cuts out at 25 km/h, so it is treated as a regular bicycle in Morocco and the EU.

## Can I ride in the rain?

Yes. The V2 frame, motor and battery are rated IP65, which means they are protected against rain and splashes. Do not submerge the bike or clean it with a pressure washer.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@voltway.example or use the chat in the Voltway app. Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 Morocco time.

## Can I buy a second battery?

Yes. The 540 Wh spare battery (part VW-BAT-540) is sold separately and is compatible with every V2 produced after January 2025.
