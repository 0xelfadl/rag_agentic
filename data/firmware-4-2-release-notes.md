# V2 Firmware 4.2 Release Notes

Released 2 September 2026 over the air through the Voltway app.

## What's new

Firmware 4.2 adds a new Eco+ assist mode, which extends range by about 15% compared with Eco by limiting peak motor torque to 35 Nm. It also improves the start-assist ramp, so the bike no longer jerks when you start pedalling in Turbo on a hill.

The display now shows the battery percentage with one decimal place when you hold the minus button for 3 seconds.

## Fixes

- Fixed the speed sensor dropping out for one second at exactly 25 km/h.
- Fixed range estimates being too optimistic in cold weather below 5 °C.
- Fixed Bluetooth reconnection after the app is force-closed.

## Known issues

Some bikes with controller hardware revision C show error E07 after updating to 4.2. The motor still works, but assist is limited to Eco until the error clears. A fix is planned for firmware 4.2.1, expected in October 2026. Until then, turning the bike off and on usually clears E07 for the rest of the ride.

You can find the controller revision in the app under Bike > About > Controller.

## How to update

Keep the battery above 30% and the app open during the update. The update takes about 10 minutes. Do not ride or turn off the bike while the display shows the update icon.
