# Voltway Warranty and Returns Policy

This policy applies to all Voltway bikes and accessories bought directly from Voltway or from an authorised dealer. It was last updated in March 2026.

## Coverage periods

The frame is covered for 5 years against manufacturing defects. The motor, battery, display and controller are covered for 2 years. Wear parts such as tyres, brake pads, chain, cassette and grips are not covered unless they fail within the first 30 days because of a manufacturing defect.

The battery warranty also guarantees capacity: if the battery holds less than 70% of its rated capacity within the 2-year period, we replace it. Capacity is measured with the diagnostic tool at an authorised service centre.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover damage from crashes, misuse, or modifications. Modifications include speed tuning devices, third-party firmware, non-Voltway chargers, and batteries that were opened. Damage from submerging the bike or from pressure washing is also excluded, because the V2 is rated IP65 and not for immersion.

Commercial use, such as delivery fleets or rentals, reduces the electrical warranty to 1 year. The frame warranty stays at 5 years.

## Firmware-related faults

Faults caused by an official Voltway firmware update are always covered, even after the warranty period has ended, for 90 days after the update was released. If an official update causes an error code, contact support before attempting any repair, because the fix is usually a further update or a free controller reset at a service centre.

## How to make a claim

1. Open the Voltway app and go to Support > Warranty claim.
2. Enter the frame number, which is printed under the bottom bracket.
3. Describe the problem and attach photos or a short video. Include any error code shown on the display.
4. Support replies within 2 business days with either a remote fix, a pickup, or an appointment at a service centre.

You need proof of purchase. If you bought from a dealer, the dealer's invoice is enough.

## Returns

You can return an unused bike within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. A bike that has been ridden more than 20 km can still be returned within 14 days, but a 10% restocking fee applies. Accessories can be returned within 30 days if they are unused and in the original packaging.

Return shipping is free within Morocco. For EU returns, the customer pays shipping unless the item is defective.

## Transferring the warranty

The warranty transfers to a second owner if the bike is registered to the new owner in the Voltway app. The coverage periods still count from the original purchase date.
